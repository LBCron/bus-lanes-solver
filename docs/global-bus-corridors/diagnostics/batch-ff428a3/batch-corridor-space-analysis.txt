Single-layer cut-capacity analysis for the original pocket family

Every connection starts at x=-3 and ends at x=3, so each must cross x=0.
At that cut, fixed copper leaves one 0.8 mm central aperture and two
exterior bypasses whose physical height is the board padding P.

Wire width w = 0.1 mm; trace clearance c = 0.1 mm;
board edge clearance e = 0.1 mm. Wire center pitch must be at least
p = w+c = 0.2 mm. A wire center must remain w/2+c = 0.15 mm from
fixed copper and w/2+e = 0.15 mm from the board edge.

For a legal center interval of length L, at most floor(L/p)+1 wire
centers fit. Angled crossings cannot increase that upper bound:
their crossing points still lie on x=0 and must meet physical clearance.

Central aperture:
  Lcenter = 0.8 - 2*(0.05+0.1) = 0.5 mm
  Ncenter <= floor(0.5/0.2)+1 = 3

Each exterior bypass with original P=2.2 mm:
  Lbypass = P - (0.05+0.1) - (0.05+0.1) = 1.9 mm
  Nbypass <= floor(1.9/0.2)+1 = 10

Total cut capacity <= 3 + 10 + 10 = 23 connections.
Thus the original 24- and 32-lane single-layer pocket fixtures are
physically infeasible under their declared geometry. Their unsuccessful
5000-yield runs do not establish a solver scalability limit. Original
16-lane routing remains within this necessary capacity bound.

Prepared capacity-expanded family uses P=max(2.2, 0.15*n+0.4) mm:
  n=16: P=2.8, Nbypass<=13, total cut capacity<=29
  n=24: P=4.0, Nbypass<=19, total cut capacity<=41
  n=32: P=5.2, Nbypass<=25, total cut capacity<=53

These expanded cases remove this particular necessary cut-capacity
obstruction. The bound does not prove that every detailed routing or
endpoint topology is feasible.

The prepared standalone harness compares baseline negotiation against a
coarse-planned strict batch proposal. Both protect every terminal point;
the batch proposal additionally fixes each earlier completed lane as hard
copper, tries at most four deduplicated orders, and permits at most 64000
A* expansions per route (500-expansion cooperative batches). It accepts
only complete endpoint connectivity and clean continuous copper DRC.
Timing/skew matching is explicitly outside this experimental harness.

No experimental work was executed during preparation. Root GO is required
through CORRIDOR_BATCH_GO=1 before either prepared script will run.
